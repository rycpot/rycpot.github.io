Extra Guac

25+ tools for the page you're on, in one side panel. We may have gotten carried away.

Available in theChrome Web Store GitHub· open source Privacy policy

What's inside

  • Screenshota dragged area, the visible tab or the full page, with an editor to mark it up, blur parts or remove the background
  • Text from imagedrag over any text on screen; it's read and copied
  • Privacy blurhides emails, phone numbers and other personal data on screen
  • Scratch padssix pads that save as you type, in rich or plain text
  • Refreshhard refresh, or nuke a site's cookies, storage and cache
  • Auto-refreshon a fixed or random timer, with an alert when your keywords appear
  • URL shortenwith x.gd, spoo.me or dub.sh, copied right away
  • QR codefor a page, link or text, static or dynamic, with your own icon in the middle
  • Tab volumeturn down one noisy tab
  • Proxyopen the sites you pick through your own Webshare proxies, in the country you choose; they see the proxy's location, time zone and language, with WebRTC off
  • Session switchkeep several logins for one site and switch between them in a click, each with its own proxy if you like
  • Awakekeep the screen on for a set time, or until you say stop
  • Highlight wordsneon marks for words you care about, on every site or per site
  • Clipboard historysearch and pin text you copy on web pages
  • Enable copy & right-clickunlock sites that block them, one site at a time
  • What color?copy the color of any pixel on the page
  • What element?copy an element's CSS or XPath selector
  • What font?find the real name of any font on the page
  • Zoom & rotate imageshold a key and scroll over any image
  • Save images on clickhold a key and click; save as is, PNG, JPG or WebP
  • Upload imagesright-click to upload to ImgLink or X02, link copied
  • Dark modeon every site, or only the ones you pick
  • Remove elementsclick away clutter; it stays gone on that site
  • Open other site URLs in new tablinks leaving a site open in a new tab, on the sites you pick
  • URL auto redirectrewrite addresses with your own rules
  • Refresh on focusreload a page when you come back to it
  • Drive backupsnapshots of your settings and data to your own Google Drive; session switch logins only if you turn that on, encrypted with your passphrase
  • Beamsend files between your devices with a 6-digit code, encrypted end to end; the other device can use Extra Guac too, or rycpot.github.io/beam in any browser

Privacy

Everything stays in your browser. There are no accounts, no analytics and no server of ours.

Data leaves your browser only when you use a tool that needs an outside service you chose (a link shortener, an image host, text recognition, a proxy, a backup to your own Google Drive, or Beam's connection service and relays, which only see your files encrypted), and it goes only to that service. Tools that record anything are off until you turn them on. Read the full privacy policy.

The code is open source (0BSD) on GitHub, so you can check all of this yourself.
